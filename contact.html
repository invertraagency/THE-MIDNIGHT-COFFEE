Frequently Asked Questions

What are your opening hours?
We are open Monday through Sunday, from 6pm to 2am. Join us for the perfect late-night coffee experience.
Do you accept table reservations?
Yes! We welcome table reservations. Simply use our reservation form or give us a call to book your table.
Do you offer private events?
Absolutely. Our café is available for private events, celebrations, and gatherings. Select "Private Event" in the inquiry type to get started.
Can I contact you about catering?
Yes! We offer catering for events of all sizes. Select "Catering" in the inquiry type and we'll get back to you with details.
